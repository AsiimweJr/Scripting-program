What it does:Jupyter Book takes your notes / code / markdown files in a folder like dir3 and turns them into a beautiful website / online book - like a textbook.Example: That question you have at the top of your screen:"(d) Explain why all the water in the tea cup turned brown when the tea bag was dipped in the hot water."If you have many questions like that, Jupyter Book can turn them all into a proper book with chapters, pages, images, that you can share online or as PDF.What those 3 commands do:sudo apt install pipx - Installs a safe installer tool for Python apps.pipx install jupyter-book - Installs the Jupyter Book app safely in its own space, so it doesn't break your Ubuntu system (that's why you got the externally-managed-environment error before).jupyter-book start - Tries to create/preview your book website.So in short: You are installing the tool that makes your notes into a website-book.Are you trying to make a Science revision book with those tea experiment questions?
